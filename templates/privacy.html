Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information

01Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the financial records you choose to enter, including expense amounts, categories, dates and notes. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and usage logs, to keep the service running reliably. Spendly does not ask for or store your bank account numbers, card details or banking passwords.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide Spendly's features: storing your expenses, showing summaries and spending insights, and keeping your account secure. We may use your email address to send important account or service notices. We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

03Data Security and Protection

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords and restricted access to stored data. No online service can guarantee complete security, so we also encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your login details private.

04Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers, such as hosting, database and email services, to operate the platform. These providers process data only on our behalf and are expected to protect it to standards consistent with this policy. We do not share your financial records with third parties except where required by law.

05User Rights

You may access, correct or export the information in your account at any time. You may also request that we delete your account and all associated data, after which it will be permanently removed from our active systems. If you have questions about your data or this policy, please contact us through the details provided on the Spendly website.
